Workshop Moderation

For blueprint workshops, process design, and optimisation sessions

One of the hardest parts of any EWM project is not the software - it's getting operations, IT and management to a shared understanding of what the warehouse should do and why. I facilitate that process: structuring the conversation, keeping it moving, and making sure the outcome is something everyone can work with.

With 15+ years of EWM project experience on both sides of the table, I can moderate with deep domain knowledge rather than just process facilitation. I understand what questions to ask, where the real conflicts tend to hide, and how to translate between operational reality and system logic.

Typical workshop formats

  • EWM blueprint workshops - scoping and designing the future warehouse processes
  • Process optimisation sessions - identifying and resolving operational pain points
  • Requirements workshops - capturing what the business needs in a form IT can act on
  • Decision workshops - aligning stakeholders on design choices with real trade-offs

What I bring as a moderator

  • Domain knowledge - I know what is and isn't possible in EWM standard
  • Neutrality - as an external consultant I have no organisational agenda
  • Structure - sessions produce concrete outputs, not just discussion
  • Experience - I've seen the same disagreements in many projects and know how to resolve them

MFS Design

For projects with automated warehouse equipment

Material Flow Systems are where SAP EWM meets the physical automation layer - and where most projects underestimate the complexity. I provide focused support for MFS implementations and extensions, working closely with the equipment supplier, the PLC engineers, and your EWM team.

Typical topics

  • MFS architecture: communication points, resources, queues, putaway and removal logic
  • EWM ↔ PLC communication: telegrams, handshakes, error handling
  • Conveyor logic, identification points, cross-docking flows
  • Crane control and high-bay warehouse design
  • Emulation and testing of automated processes

Engagement model

  • On-site at the customer or the integrator
  • Remote support for spec writing, code review, troubleshooting
  • Hands-on commissioning support during go-live

Classroom Training

For project teams, key users and consulting practices

Structured trainings for teams that need to ramp up quickly on SAP EWM (MFS) - delivered on-site, anywhere in the world, in English or in German. Hands-on with real system examples, not slides full of bullet points.

Available courses

  • EWM Best Practices - SAP's Best-Practice processes from inbound to physical inventory to production integration

Format

  • Detailed agenda and learning outcomes aligned with you and provided up front
