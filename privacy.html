Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who is responsible

This website is operated by Hendrik Schulte-Bahrenberg, a freelance SAP EWM consultant based in Germany. For the full legal contact details, please refer to the imprint. If you have any questions about how your data is handled, you can reach out via email or LinkedIn.

2. What data is collected and why

This website collects data in the following ways:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — only if you accept cookies. GA4 collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time on page, and approximate geographic region. This data is used solely to understand which content is useful to visitors. No personal profiles are built and no data is sold to third parties.
  • Server log files — when you visit the site, GitHub Pages (the hosting provider) processes standard server log data such as IP address, user agent, and timestamp. This data is used by GitHub to operate and protect the service.
  • Email contact — if you contact me by email, the content of your message and your email address are stored for the purpose of replying and, where appropriate, for ongoing project communication.

3. Cookies

This website uses a single cookie consent mechanism. If you decline cookies, no tracking cookies are set and Google Analytics is not loaded at all. Your choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so you are not asked again on future visits.

If you previously accepted cookies and want to withdraw consent, you can clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time. The next visit will treat you as a new visitor and ask again.

4. Google Analytics

If — and only if — you accept cookies, this site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a service provided by Google LLC. Data is transmitted to Google servers, which may be located in the United States. Google processes this data according to their own Privacy Policy. IP anonymization is enabled.

5. Google Fonts

This site uses the Inter font family loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When you visit a page, your browser may transmit your IP address to Google in order to fetch the font files. The font is loaded for visual consistency only and is required for the site to render as intended. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

6. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. GitHub processes technical access data (such as IP address) as part of operating the service. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

7. External links

This site contains links to third-party websites such as wmexperts.online, LinkedIn, and YouTube. I have no control over how those services process your data once you follow a link. Please review their respective privacy policies.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data held about you, as well as the right to object to processing or request its restriction. To exercise these rights, please contact me via email or LinkedIn. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
